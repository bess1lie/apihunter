# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in apihunter a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming language, being respectful, and focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to bess1iework@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
